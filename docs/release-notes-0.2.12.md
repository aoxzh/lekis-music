月兔音乐（Lekis Music）0.2.12 预览版，继续打磨桌面迷你播放器：可以直接拖动进度、滚轮调音量，并在小窗里设置睡眠定时。有问题欢迎[提 issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues)。

## 新增

- **迷你播放器可拖动进度**：底部细进度条支持点按和水平拖动定位，不必展开完整窗口。
- **迷你播放器滚轮调音量**：鼠标停在迷你播放器上滚动即可调整音量。
- **迷你播放器睡眠定时**：标题行新增月亮按钮，可直接设置或关闭睡眠定时。
- 包含 0.2.11 的扫描并行读标签、0.2.10 的「放完这首」淡出和 0.2.9 的 SQLite 大库优化。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.12 preview release, continuing to polish the desktop mini player: seek directly, change volume with the wheel, and set a sleep timer without expanding. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Seekable mini player**: tap or drag the thin progress line at the bottom to seek without expanding the full window.
- **Wheel volume**: scroll over the mini player to change the volume.
- **Sleep timer in the mini player**: a moon button in the title row sets or cancels the timer.
- Includes the 0.2.11 parallel tag reads, the 0.2.10 End-of-Track fade, and the 0.2.9 SQLite large-library work.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
