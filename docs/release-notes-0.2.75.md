- 包含 0.2.74 的图片缓存管理、0.2.73 的队列下载管理和 0.2.72 的复制队列。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.75 preview release, adding copy-playlist-as-text. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Copy a playlist as text**: regular and smart playlists gain Copy Playlist in their More menu, copying a numbered "Title — Artist" list for sharing or backup.
- Includes the 0.2.74 image-cache management, 0.2.73 queue download management and 0.2.72 copy queue.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
