- 包含 0.2.58 的歌单总时长、0.2.57 的批量播放已下载和 0.2.56 的下载整行点击播放。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.59 preview release, showing total durations on album and artist pages. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Album and artist total duration**: album details show the summed running time next to the year and song count, and artist pages show it below the name (for example "12 songs · 48m"); playlists now share the same wording.
- Includes the 0.2.58 playlist duration, 0.2.57 bulk-play downloads and 0.2.56 tap-a-download-to-play.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
