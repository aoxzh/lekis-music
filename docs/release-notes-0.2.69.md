- 包含 0.2.68 的歌单内搜索、0.2.67 的反转/随机和 0.2.66 的复制智能歌单。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.69 preview release, adding play/shuffle actions to the home shelves. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Play or shuffle Recently Played / Most Played**: both shelves gain compact actions in the header, while Recently Played keeps its See All link.
- Includes the 0.2.68 playlist search, 0.2.67 reverse/shuffle order and 0.2.66 smart-playlist duplication.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
