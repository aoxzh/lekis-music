## 新增

- **歌单排序**：普通歌单的更多菜单新增「排序歌单」，可按标题、艺人、专辑或添加时间重新排列并保存顺序；带确认与进度，失败会报告数量。
- 包含 0.2.64 的下载进度节流、0.2.63 的下载搜索和 0.2.62 的下载分组缓存。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.65 preview release, adding playlist sorting. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Sort a playlist**: regular playlists gain Sort Playlist in their More menu. Choose title, artist, album, or date added; the new order is saved with a confirmation and progress dialog, and failed moves are reported.
- Includes the 0.2.64 throttled download progress, 0.2.63 download search and 0.2.62 cached download groups.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
