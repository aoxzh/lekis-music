## 新增

- **流派页下载全部歌曲**：流派详情页的播放/随机旁边新增下载按钮，分页收集该流派全部歌曲后一次性加入离线下载队列。
- 包含 0.2.51 的下载列表筛选、0.2.50 的搜索结果一键播放和 0.2.49 的队列曲目添加到歌单。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.52 preview release, adding download-all for genres. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Download every song in a genre**: the genre detail header gains a download button next to Play / Shuffle; it pages through the whole genre and queues the songs for offline use in one go.
- Includes the 0.2.51 downloads filters, 0.2.50 play-all search results and 0.2.49 queue-to-playlist.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
