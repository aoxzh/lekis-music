月兔音乐（Lekis Music）0.2.50 预览版，搜索结果可以一键播放或随机播放。有问题欢迎[提 issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues)。

## 新增

- **搜索结果一键播放**：歌曲分组标题旁新增「播放全部」和「随机播放」按钮，命中很多歌曲时不用逐首点；筛选到「歌曲」后同样可用。
- 包含 0.2.49 的队列曲目添加到歌单、0.2.48 的艺人页展开全部歌曲和 0.2.47 的下载艺人全部歌曲。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.50 preview release, adding play-all / shuffle-all to search results. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Play search results in one tap**: the Songs section header now has Play All and Shuffle All buttons, and they stay available after filtering to Songs.
- Includes the 0.2.49 queue-to-playlist, 0.2.48 expandable artist songs and 0.2.47 download-all-for-artist.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
