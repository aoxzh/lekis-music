月兔音乐（Lekis Music）0.2.97 预览版，筛选后的下载结果可以直接播放。有问题欢迎[提 issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues)。

## 新增

- **下载筛选后直接播放**：筛选/搜索到已下载曲目后，标签右侧出现播放和随机按钮，可直接把当前结果加入队列。
- 包含 0.2.96 的播放历史缓存、0.2.95 的下载按专辑排序和 0.2.94 的播放历史搜索与复制。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.97 preview release, playing filtered download results directly. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Play filtered downloads**: when the search/filter narrows the completed downloads, Play and Shuffle buttons next to the chips queue exactly those results.
- Includes the 0.2.96 history cache, 0.2.95 downloads-by-album sort and 0.2.94 history search/copy.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
