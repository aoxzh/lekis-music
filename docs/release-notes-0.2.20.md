月兔音乐（Lekis Music）0.2.20 预览版，队列长按菜单现在可以直接打开所属专辑或艺人页。有问题欢迎[提 issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues)。

## 新增

- **队列长按菜单直达专辑 / 艺人**：长按队列曲目除了喜欢、排序和移除，还能直接打开所属专辑或艺人页；菜单只在对应信息存在时显示，不会出现空入口。
- 包含 0.2.19 的队列长按收藏/排序/移除，以及 0.2.18 的睡眠定时倒计时。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.20 preview release, letting the queue long-press menu jump straight to the track's album or artist. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Album / artist from the queue menu**: long-pressing a queue row can now open its album or artist page in addition to like, reorder and remove. Entries only appear when the track actually has that information.
- Includes the 0.2.19 queue long-press favorite/reorder/remove actions and the 0.2.18 sleep-timer countdown.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
