## 新增

- **歌单总时长**：歌单详情的副标题现在显示曲目总时长（例如「24 首 · 1 小时 12 分」），不再显示「可上下移动条目」的提示。
- 包含 0.2.57 的批量播放已下载、0.2.56 的下载整行点击播放和 0.2.55 的歌曲库一键播放。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.58 preview release, showing a playlist's total duration. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Playlist total duration**: a playlist header now shows the sum of its track durations (for example "24 songs · 1h 12m") instead of the reorder hint.
- Includes the 0.2.57 bulk-play downloads, 0.2.56 tap-a-download-to-play and 0.2.55 library play-all.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
