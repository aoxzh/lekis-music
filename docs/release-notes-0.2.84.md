## English

Lekis Music 0.2.84 preview release, adding a "More by artist" shelf to album pages. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **More by artist on album pages**: the bottom of an album now shows a horizontal shelf of other albums by the same artist, linking straight to them; it only appears when such albums exist.
- Includes the 0.2.83 album/artist copy track list, 0.2.82 open-download-with-app and 0.2.81 Top Tracks actions.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
