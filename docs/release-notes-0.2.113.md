月兔音乐（Lekis Music）0.2.113 预览版，喜欢的歌曲可以保存成歌单。有问题欢迎[提 issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues)。

## 新增

- **喜欢的歌曲保存为歌单**：喜欢页可把全部收藏保存成普通歌单，分页收集后带命名、进度和失败数量。
- 包含 0.2.112 的播放历史保存为歌单、0.2.111 的喜欢页整组下载和 0.2.110 的喜欢页随机播放。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.113 preview release, letting you save Liked Songs as a playlist. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Save Liked Songs as a playlist**: the Liked Songs page can turn every favorite into a regular playlist, collecting them in pages with naming, progress and a failure count.
- Includes the 0.2.112 history save-as-playlist, 0.2.111 Liked Songs batch download and 0.2.110 Liked Songs shuffle.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
