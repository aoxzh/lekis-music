## English

Lekis Music 0.2.95 preview release, adding album sorting to downloads. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Sort downloads by album**: the sort menu can group downloads by album, then by artist and title within an album.
- Includes the 0.2.94 playback-history search/copy, 0.2.93 queue move-to-top/bottom and 0.2.92 queue find.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
