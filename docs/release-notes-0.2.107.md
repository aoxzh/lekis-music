月兔音乐（Lekis Music）0.2.107 预览版，歌单可以合并了。有问题欢迎[提 issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues)。

## 新增

- **歌单合并**：普通歌单可以把另一张歌单（含智能歌单）的曲目按顺序追加到末尾，带选择、确认和进度。
- 包含 0.2.106 的队列移除重复曲目、0.2.105 的分页预取和 0.2.104 的打乱未播放曲目。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.107 preview release, adding playlist merging. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Merge playlists**: a regular playlist can append the tracks of another playlist (smart playlists included) in order, with picker, confirmation, and progress.
- Includes the 0.2.106 queue duplicate removal, 0.2.105 page prefetch and 0.2.104 shuffle-upcoming action.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
