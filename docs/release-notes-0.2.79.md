## English

Lekis Music 0.2.79 preview release, making the top artists and albums in stats open their pages. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Stats page navigation**: Top Artists and Top Albums are now tappable and open the artist/album page; albums are matched by title and artist against the local library.
- Includes the 0.2.78 playlist move-to-top/bottom, 0.2.77 downloads sorting and 0.2.76 show-in-folder.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
