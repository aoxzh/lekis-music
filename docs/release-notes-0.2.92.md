- 包含 0.2.91 的专辑/艺人详情缓存、0.2.90 的时长统计缓存和 0.2.89 的歌词来源标识。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.92 preview release, adding find-and-jump inside the playback queue. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Find in the playback queue**: the queue More menu can search by title or artist across queue pages and scroll to the first match, with a notice when nothing matches.
- Includes the 0.2.91 album/artist detail cache, 0.2.90 duration cache and 0.2.89 lyrics source badge.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
