月兔音乐（Lekis Music）0.2.73 预览版，播放队列的曲目菜单可以直接管理下载。有问题欢迎[提 issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues)。

## 新增

- **队列行下载管理**：播放队列的曲目菜单新增下载/取消/重试/删除下载，直接在当前队列完成离线管理。
- 包含 0.2.72 的复制队列、0.2.71 的正在播放查看专辑/艺人和 0.2.70 的播放历史一键播放。

## English

Lekis Music 0.2.73 preview release, adding download management to queue rows. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/aoxzh/lekis-music/issues) for problems.

**New**

- **Download management from the queue**: a queue row's menu can download, cancel, retry, or remove the offline copy without leaving the queue.
- Includes the 0.2.72 copy queue, 0.2.71 Now Playing album/artist links and 0.2.70 playback-history actions.

**Downloads:** Android arm64 APK; Windows x64 installer or portable ZIP; macOS arm64 DMG or ZIP; Linux x64 AppImage or tar.gz. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
